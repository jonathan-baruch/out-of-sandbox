The Migration

For the last few years, my world has been defined by system stability and the pursuit of the “perfect test” for the Vision Pro. Everything is controlled, simulated, and reproducible.

But three weeks in Patagonia is the opposite of a controlled environment. There are no mocks for 60mph winds in Torres del Paine, and you can’t “revert to a previous build” when you’re deep in the backcountry.

System Specs:

  • Location: Southern Patagonia, Chile
  • Uptime: 21 days
  • Latency: High (Zero bars of LTE)
  • Build Status: Success

Moving Out of Sandbox isn’t just about travel; it’s about testing the human experience in an unoptimized environment. Over the next few posts, I’ll be sharing the “logs” of this journey—the bugs, the breakthroughs, and the beauty of life at the edges.

Stay tuned for the full breakdown of the trek.
